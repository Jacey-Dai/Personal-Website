How to explore

Drag the island to walk around it; scroll or pinch to move closer. The arrow keys and the ↺ ↻ buttons turn it too.

Hover or focus a building to see its name. Press Enter to step inside.

On a touchscreen, tap once to identify a building, then tap again to enter. Quick navigation opens a room directly.

Lights and flags remember the places you’ve explored on this browser.
